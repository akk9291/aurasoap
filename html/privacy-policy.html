1. Information We Collect

At Aura Soaps ("www.aurasoaps.com"), we collect personal information necessary to process orders, fulfill wholesale requests, and improve your user experience:

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized for core operational purposes:

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies to maintain shopping cart sessions, analyze website traffic via secure analytics, and remember user preferences. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Security

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. Data is shared exclusively with verified logistics providers (shipping carriers) and secure payment processors to complete transactions.
