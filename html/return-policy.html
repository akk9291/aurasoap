30-Day Guarantee & Returns

We stand behind the quality of our crafted soaps. Your satisfaction is guaranteed.

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

If you are not completely satisfied with your unopened retail purchase within 30 days of delivery, you may return the items for a full refund or product exchange.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

In the rare event that your soap box arrives damaged during transit, please email photos to info@aurasoaps.com within 48 hours. We will immediately dispatch a replacement order free of charge.

Hygiene & Safety Exclusions

Due to strict personal care hygiene regulations, soap bars that have been unwrapped, used, or exposed to moisture cannot be returned unless verified as a manufacturing defect.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact customer support via email (info@aurasoaps.com) or phone (+250-795 602 083).
  2. Provide your order reference number and reason for return.
  3. Pack items securely in original eco-packaging and mail to our returns facility. Refunds process within 3 business days of receipt.
