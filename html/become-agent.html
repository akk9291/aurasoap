Principal Agent Requirements

To become a Principal Agent (PA), a Business or Business Person needs to meet the following requirements:

1. Sign a Contract
Sign a Principal Agent (PA) Contract.
2. Strategic Location
Must be located in the specified areas (Province/District/Town).
3. Outlets & Storage
Have shops, warehouses, or clients with shops/warehouses that are product end-users.
4. Flexible Payment Terms
Ability to pay 60% Cash on Delivery (CoD) and 40% within 30 days.
5. Minimum Order Quantity
Meet a Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) requirement.
6. Complete Application
Fill out the Principal Agent Application Form.
7. Aura Soaps Approval
Subject to official Aura Soaps approval following a meeting and facility visit.
Important Note: Principal Agents in neighboring Great Lakes Markets do not qualify for credit terms. They pay 100% before delivery.
