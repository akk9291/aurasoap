Essential Personal Care

Manufacturing & Distribution Excellence

AURA SOAPS specializes in the manufacturing and distribution of essential PERSONAL CARE, SANITATION, and HYGIENE products in Rwanda, Eastern DRC, and South – West Uganda. We target Household Consumers, Retail Chains (Supermarkets, Convenience Stores, and Small Grocery Stores), and many other bulk buyers like Pharmacies, Salons, and Hotels.

Our product catalog includes well-packaged Bar Soaps (Laundry & Bath), 1 or 2-Ply Luxury Toilet Paper Rolls (10-Packs), Kitchen Hand Towels (4-Packs), and a Luxurious Antiperspirant/Deodorant Combo Rollon (our signature luxury product). Later, Aura Soaps plans to add Liquid Soaps (Hand wash, Shower Gel, All-purpose Cleaner, and Dish wash).

Artisanal Process

The Art of Cold-Process Saponification

Unlike commercial soap manufacturers that boil out natural glycerin to sell separately, our eco-facility employs a 6-week slow cold-cure process.

  • Cold Blend Saponification: Oils mixed at low temperatures to protect essential fatty acids and botanical antioxidants.
  • 6-Week Natural Cure: Soap bars cure naturally in climate-controlled rooms to ensure a long-lasting, hard bar with rich lather.
  • Hand Beveled & Inspected: Every bar is individual hand-cut, beveled, and quality-tested for purity.
