Bathing Ritual Guide

  1. Lather soap bar under warm water until a thick, creamy foam develops.
  2. Gently massage foam onto body and face in smooth circular motions.
  3. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.
  4. To extend the life of your bar, store on a well-draining wooden soap dish away from direct shower water.
